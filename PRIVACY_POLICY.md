# Privacy Policy for Session Handoff

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026

Session Handoff ("the Extension") is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when you use the Extension.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
**We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data.** 
Session Handoff operates 100% locally within your browser. 

- All operations, including the extraction of conversational data from supported platforms (like Claude.ai and ChatGPT), happen entirely on your machine.
- No data is ever sent to any remote servers, APIs, analytics providers, or third parties.

## 2. Local Storage
The Extension uses your browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API to save your "snapshots" (conversation handoff briefs). 
- This data never leaves your device unless you manually copy and paste it or export it.
- You can view, edit, or clear this data at any time through the Extension's interface or by clearing your browser data.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Extension interacts with the Document Object Model (DOM) of the supported AI chat websites (e.g., Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's ChatGPT) purely to read the text you are actively viewing. It does not interface with their backend APIs, and it does not inject advertisements or trackers into your sessions.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
Because the Extension does not collect data and operates locally, we do not anticipate needing major changes to this policy. However, if significant changes are made to how the Extension operates, we will update this policy accordingly.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the project's repository.
